About OCI

A lifelong bond with India.

The Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Scheme, introduced in August 2005, enables Persons of Indian Origin to maintain a lasting connection with India. An OCI card grants lifelong multi-entry, multi-purpose visa to visit India and exempts holders from local police reporting for any length of stay.

Eligibility

Who can register as an OCI cardholder?

Per Section 7A, Citizenship Act 1955.

  • Former citizens of India Anyone who was a citizen of India on or after 26 January 1950, or was eligible to become one on that date.
  • Descendants A child, grandchild, or great-grandchild of such a citizen.
  • Minor children A minor child of an eligible person, or a minor child whose parents are both Indian citizens (or one of whom is).
  • Spouses of eligible persons Spouses of Indian citizens or OCI cardholders, provided the marriage has been registered and subsisted for at least two years.
  • Pre-partition territories Those who belonged to a territory that became part of India after 15 August 1947.
Please note

Persons whose parents, grandparents, or great-grandparents are or have been citizens of Pakistan, Bangladesh, or other notified countries are not eligible for OCI registration.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Who is eligible to register as an OCI cardholder?

Any person of full age and capacity who was a citizen of India on or after 26 January 1950, was eligible to become one on that date, or belonged to a territory that became part of India after 15 August 1947 — and their children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren. Spouses of eligible persons may also apply, subject to a minimum two-year marriage requirement.

Who was eligible to become a citizen of India on 26.01.1950?

Persons domiciled in the territory of India who were born in India, or either of whose parents was born in India, or who had been ordinarily resident in India for not less than five years immediately before the commencement of the Constitution.

Which territories became part of India after 15.08.1947?

Territories that merged with the Union of India after independence, including Sikkim, Pondicherry, Dadra & Nagar Haveli, Goa, Daman & Diu, from their respective dates of merger.

Can the spouse of an eligible person apply?

Yes, provided the marriage has been registered and has subsisted for a continuous period of not less than two years immediately preceding the application. The spouse is subject to prior security clearance by a competent authority in India.

Can minor children apply for OCI registration?

Yes. A minor child of an eligible person, or a minor child whose both parents are citizens of India or one of whose parents is a citizen of India, is eligible.

Where do I apply and which form do I use?

Applications are submitted online on this portal. After submission, supporting documents are verified at the Indian Mission / FRRO / authorised outsource agency in your country of residence.
